# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good faith, give and receive actionable feedback, and prioritize the safety and privacy of people affected by the systems built with this project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers. All reports will be reviewed and handled fairly and confidentially.
