## Deliberate design

The existing kernel remains generic and fail-closed. CX0 adds typed context around it rather than embedding healthcare, trading, RAG, or framework-specific branches. Model output and retrieved content are explicitly proposals or evidence candidates, not authority. The first implementation therefore focuses on identity, canonicalization, provenance, trust, freshness, and attenuation.

## Implementation

1. Added `WorkflowRunContext` and `WorkflowBudgets` with inherited tenant/root-agent identity, step lineage, deadline, kill-switch epoch, policy snapshot, and attenuation checks.
2. Added `ToolIntent` with versioned schema, canonical arguments, resource scope, destination, idempotency, side-effect mode, provenance, and deterministic hash.
3. Added `EvidenceEnvelope` with source, tenant scope, content hash, provenance, freshness, trust, classification, lineage, citations, and redacted reference.
4. Added `DecisionRecord` and `ControlEvent` contracts for future immutable decision and event persistence.
5. Documented compatibility strategy, trust boundaries, adversarial threats, and the CX0 state-transition model.
