## Failures discovered

No failing regression remained after implementation. The main product risk remains unvalidated customer behavior: synthetic simulation can prove mechanics but cannot prove willingness to pay, retention, or acceptable review burden.

## Decisions and trade-offs

A local JSONL event log was chosen to preserve reproducibility and avoid premature analytics infrastructure. The dry-run simulator deliberately cannot produce an execution handoff. A dashboard is deferred until two pilots establish stable event definitions.
